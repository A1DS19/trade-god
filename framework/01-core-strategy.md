# Core Strategy Definition

Source: https://www.skool.com/coinpicksgenesis/classroom/eed2ad74?md=dbaffd895ea8412a8671bbe4f7cbcc93

At the heart of our approach is a focused principle:

We seek coins with low liquidity relative to the current market cycle that show clear potential to evolve into high-liquidity positions.

This is how asymmetric opportunities are created.

Liquidity isn't just data it's direction. It reveals where capital is and where it's going. Instead of chasing momentum, we position early before the repricing.

We look for underexposed assets with strong fundamentals, credible infrastructure, and narrative alignment. The goal is to anticipate where liquidity will rotate next and move before the crowd.

This is the lens we apply to every asset in our system.

It's not noise. It's the framework.
