# Team Credibility Scoring System

Source: https://www.skool.com/coinpicksgenesis/classroom/eed2ad74?md=0e32e8d435954e15988a4880aa42b9dc

This system helps evaluate the credibility of any crypto team by scoring the top **3–5 most important individuals**, then applying a **founder weight multiplier** to reflect real-world execution risk and leadership value.

---

## ✅ Step 1: Identify the Key Team Members (3 to 5 People)

Look for the top contributors in Product and Marketing, plus the Founder. These are usually the ones responsible for making the company work in the real world.

Your 3–5 key people will typically include:

- **Founder** – The primary visionary and decision-maker (can also be the lead dev)
- **Head of Product** – The person most responsible for building and executing the product vision (usually a lead developer or technical founder)
- **Head of Marketing** – The person driving demand, partnerships, branding, and user acquisition
- **Optional #4–5** – Any standout execs, co-founders, or uniquely valuable contributors (based on traction or background)

> Note: Titles can vary — focus on **function and influence**, not just what their LinkedIn says.

---

## ✅ Step 2: Score Each Person (0–10 Points Each)

Use the 3-part scoring model to assign an individual score to each of the selected team members.

### 1. High Credibility (H Score – max 5 points)
- 5 – Founded and scaled a successful crypto company with real traction
- 4 – Leadership in a global institution (e.g. SEC, CFTC, IMF, BIS, G7/G20)
- 4 – Executive at a major crypto company (e.g. Coinbase, Binance, Kraken)
- 2 – Founded a crypto project with unclear or early traction
- 0 – No founding or executive leadership

### 2. Medium Credibility (M Score – max 3 points)
- 3 – Founded a real-world business with profit, growth, or acquisition
- 2 – Non-executive role at a top company (e.g. PM at Coinbase, engineer at Amazon)
- 1 – Business exists, but traction is unclear
- 0 – No real-world business experience

### 3. Low Credibility (L Score – max 2 points)
- 2 – Holds strong credentials (MBA, PhD, CFA, etc.)
- 1 – Has exposure (e.g. influencer or public face) but lacks real roles or skills
- 0 – No experience, credentials, or evidence of contribution

➡️ **Individual Score = H + M + L (Max 10 points)**

---

## Step by Step Team Member Analysis

**First:** write a **ONE-SENTENCE** summary explanation of the main team member's past experience **outside this project**, and provide direct source links to prove what you said (with "quotations" to Ctrl + F your specific fact on that linked page).

**Critical rule:** When evaluating a team member, do **not** use the current project's success as proof of credibility — especially since most of the projects we're analyzing are still small and unproven. Instead, focus on **past experiences** and look for evidence of skills or achievements that could realistically translate into making this project succeed.

**For every prior role or company listed, you must include:**
1. **At least one financial metric first** (e.g., revenue, valuation, market cap of prior crypto project, total funding raised)
2. **An adoptive metric** if available (e.g., monthly active users, total downloads, employees, client base)

If both types of data exist, include both. This grounds the team's credibility in real-world performance numbers, not in vague titles or in the project they are currently building.

**ONLY USE ONE SENTENCE.** If you truly understand their skillset, you can convey it in one sentence. The 1-page research PDF carries the longer history.

**Grade format:** `5 / 5 + 3 / 3 + 2 / 2 = 10 / 10`

Repeat for team members 2 through 5.

---

## ✅ Step 3: Weight the Founder 5× More Than Others

**Part 1:** Multiply the founder's score by 5, then add the individual scores of the other team members.

**Part 2:** Divide that sum by the total number of scores used. The founder counts as 5 scores; every other member counts as 1.

### 🔢 Example

- Founder Score: 8
- Head of Product: 7
- Head of Marketing: 5
- Strategic Advisor: 6

**Step 1 (Weighted Sum):** (8 × 5) + 7 + 5 + 6 = 40 + 18 = 58

**Step 2 (Divide by total scores used):** 58 ÷ (5 + 3) = 58 ÷ 8 = **7.25 Final Score**

---

## Note on Advisors

Advisors should only be listed as a separate "member" when they are **world-class** (ultimate outliers) and actually involved in the project — not just "advisor" in name. When this happens (rarely), include them as Team Member #5 and be ultra-concise.
