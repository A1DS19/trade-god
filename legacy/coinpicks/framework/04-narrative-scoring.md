## Demand Framework: How Coins Get Purchased & Increase In Price

**Core Definition:**

Demand is the desire of the market to buy a project, and it is driven primarily by narrative.

At its core, demand is how many people want to buy not because of utility or fundamentals alone, but because of the story they believe. **In crypto, story = value.**

## Narrative Scoring System

The final one-page report uses **six sub-scores** totaling **/31**. The Skool framework page lists 5 (totaling /25), but the **One Page Report Submission framework** and the worked Sogni example include **Hair-on-Fire Innovation (0–6)** as a 6th score that lives inside the Narrative section.

| Sub-score | Max |
|---|---|
| Narrative Maturity | 7 |
| Smart Money Compatibility (a.k.a. Smart Money Fit) | 6 |
| Hair-on-Fire Innovation | 6 |
| Narrative Communication | 5 |
| Narrative Lineage | 4 |
| Narrative Mutation | 3 |
| **Total** | **/31** |

---

### 1. Narrative Maturity Score (0–7)
**How far along is the narrative in the market cycle?**

### 2. Smart Money Compatibility Score (0–6)
**Would serious capital flow into this structure?**

### 3. Hair-on-Fire Innovation Score (0–6)
Does this product innovate or solve a mass problem in a way that users and/or the entire industry urgently feels they must adopt right now? (Migrated from Product framework into Narrative section in the final report.)

### 4. Narrative Communication Score (0–5)
**How well is the story being told?**

### 5. Narrative Lineage Score (0–4)
**Is this clearly connected to a previously proven 100x trend?**

### 6. Narrative Mutation Score (0–3)
**Has this narrative evolved into a real-world trend?**

---

## Evidence Standard

For every sub-score, include a **1–2 sentence explanation backed by a source link with a Ctrl+F-able quote**.

Format: `https://example.com "exact quote to Ctrl+F on the page"`
